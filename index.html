01 · Flagship

AI Video Editing Pipeline

One command turns raw footage into a finished YouTube video or Short. Whisper transcribes on the GPU. A rough-cut engine removes fillers, stutters and retakes. Then the pipeline adds motion graphics, captions, sound effects and music ducking, and masters the audio to -14 LUFS. It can also reframe the video to 9:16 around the speaker's face.

Python · faster-whisper · FFmpeg · HyperFrames · GSAP · OpenCV

How I build

Claude plans. The machine does the heavy work.

I run Claude Code like a small team. That keeps my tools fast, private and cheap to run.

  1. 01

    Custom skills

    Reusable skills for jobs I repeat. For example, an /edit-video skill runs the whole editing pipeline with very few credits.

  2. 02

    MCP tools and connectors

    Claude works with real tools: market data, live charts, the browser, Google Drive and Canva.

  3. 03

    Project memory

    CLAUDE.md files and a memory store, so every session starts with the full context.

  4. 04

    Local heavy lifting

    Whisper on the GPU, FFmpeg with NVENC and local AI models all run offline.

  5. 05

    Automatic checks

    Before anything counts as done, scripts check length, loudness, audio sync and page counts, and render previews.

About

A builder who used to teach.

I'm Carl Owen Belen, and most people call me Owen. I'm an AI Engineer from Pangasinan, Philippines, and I've been coding since 2015. These days I build AI tools with Claude Code: agents, automations and media pipelines that take busywork off people's plates.

From 2025 to 2026 I taught Web Development and Security as a College Instructor at the University of Eastern Pangasinan, with Claude, Gemini and NotebookLM built into my lessons. Teaching changed how I build. When you have to walk a room of students through your work, you stop hiding behind clever tricks.

I'm a Civil Service Professional passer, and I'm taking my Master in Information Technology (Cybersecurity). Outside work, I trade the markets with my own bots and keep company with my dog Ginger and my cats Pepper and Tabby. My partner is based in Manila, and her dog Killer comes along on our weekend trips.
